💡 Real-Life Usage Scenarios
📌 Scenario 1: Pehli Baar Setup Karna
text
1. Bot start karein (terminal me QR scan karein)

2. Apne "Message Yourself" chat me bhejein:
   /selfchat 923001234567
   
3. Confirmation aayegi:
   ✅ Self-Chat Protection SET!
   
4. Ab test karein — bhejein:
   /list
   
5. ✅ Rate list aayegi aur WAHI RAHEGI (delete nahi hogi)
📌 Scenario 2: Naya Product Add Karna
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /addproduct usocket | Universal Socket (10A) | Qty batao
   
2. Confirm reply aayegi (self chat me rahegi)
3. Customer poochega "Universal Socket ka rate?" 
   → AI full name bolega: "Universal Socket (10A)"
📌 Scenario 3: VIP Customer Ko Discount Dena
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /customrate 923001234567 wifi 4000
   
2. Us customer (923001234567) ko message karein "Wifi ka rate?"
   → AI bolega: "Rs. 4000" (special rate)
   
3. Baaki customers ko normal "Rs. 4,500" batayega
📌 Scenario 4: Rate Change Karna
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /rate wifi 5000
   
2. Ab sab customers ko naya rate "Rs. 5000" batayega
   (Except VIP customers jinke custom rate set hain)
📌 Scenario 5: Kisi Customer Ka Bot Band Karna
text
1. Us customer ke chat me (aap ke WhatsApp se) bhejein:
   off
   
2. Us chat me AI reply dena band kar dega
3. Baaki customers ko normal reply karta rahega

4. Dobara chalu karne ke liye:
   on
📌 Scenario 6: Group Me Bot Activate Karna
text
Default: Groups silent hain

1. Group me bhejein:
   gon
   → Command auto-delete ho jayegi
   → Ab us group me AI replies dega

2. Band karne ke liye:
   goff
⚠️ Command Tips (Zaroori Baatein)
✅ Space sahi rakhein:

/rate wifi 4500 (theek hai)

/ratewifi4500 (galat)

✅ Pipe | zaroori hai /addproduct me:

/addproduct key | Name | Price

✅ Numbers normalize ho jaate hain:

0300..., +92300..., 92300... — sab chalega

✅ Cancel ke 3 alfaaz:

cancel, reset, delete — koi bhi chalega

✅ Self chat me test karein pehle, phir customer pe use karein

✅ /selfchat command sabse pehle set karein — iske baad hi aap apne "You" chat me baaki commands use karein

🆘 Common Problems & Solutions
Problem	Solution
Commands delete ho rahe hain "You" chat me	/selfchat <apna_number> bhejein
Customer ko naya rate nahi dikh raha	/customrate <number> <key> <price> bhejein
Naya product AI nahi bata raha	/addproduct se add karein, phir test karein
Bot reply nahi de raha	on bhejein us chat me
Group me reply nahi kar raha	gon bhejein group me
Purane rates ghalat ho gaye	/seed bhejein
