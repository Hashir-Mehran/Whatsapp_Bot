🔑 Bot Me Use Hone Wali SAARI "/" Commands (Keys)
Achha bhai, samajh gaya! Aap slash (/) wali commands ki baat kar rahe hain — jo bot me type karke use hoti hain. Yeh rahi poori list ek jagah:

📋 Complete Commands List (Saari / Wali Keys)
👤 1. Owner Commands — Apne "You" Chat Me
(Yahan kuch bhi delete nahi hoga — sab visible rahega)

#	Command	Format	Kaam
1	/list	/list	Poori rate list dekho
2	/rates	/rates	Same as /list (alias)
3	/ratelist	/ratelist	Same as /list (alias)
4	/newlist	/newlist	Last 30 din ke naye products
5	/newproducts	/newproducts	Same as /newlist (alias)
6	/addproduct	/addproduct key | Full Name | Price	Naya product add karo
7	/rate	/rate key newprice	Kisi product ka rate update karo
8	/rename	/rename key New Full Name	Product ka naam badlo
9	/customrate	/customrate 923001234567 key price	VIP special rate set karo
10	/customrate	/customrate 923001234567 key cancel	VIP rate hataao
11	/customlist	/customlist	Saare VIP rates dekho
12	/seed	/seed	Default rates DB me reset karo
🟢 2. Non-Slash Commands (Owner)
#	Command	Kaam
1	off	Specific chat me bot pause karo
2	stop	Same as off
3	on	Chat me bot resume karo
4	start	Same as on
5	gon	Group me bot ON
6	groupchaton	Same as gon
7	goff	Group me bot OFF
8	groupchatoff	Same as goff

Group commands = Hamesha delete honge (taakay kisi ko pata na chale)

💡 Real-Life Usage Scenarios
Scenario 1: Naya Product Add Karna
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /addproduct usocket | Universal Socket (10A) | Qty batao
   
2. Confirm reply aayegi (self chat me rahegi)
3. Customer poochega "Universal Socket ka rate?" 
   → AI full name bolega: "Universal Socket (10A)"
Scenario 2: VIP Customer Ko Discount Dena
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /customrate 923001234567 wifi 4000
   
2. Us customer (923001234567) ko message karein "Wifi ka rate?"
   → AI bolega: "Rs. 4000" (special rate)
   
3. Baaki customers ko normal "Rs. 4,500" batayega
Scenario 3: Rate Change Karna
text
1. Apne chat me bhejein:
   /rate wifi 5000
   
2. Ab sab customers ko naya rate "Rs. 5000" batayega
   (Except VIP customers jinke custom rate set hain)
Scenario 4: Kisi Customer Ka Bot Band Karna
text
1. Us customer ke chat me (aap ke WhatsApp se) bhejein:
   off
   
2. Us chat me AI reply dena band kar dega
3. Baaki customers ko normal reply karta rahega

4. Dobara chalu karne ke liye:
   on
Scenario 5: Group Me Bot Activate Karna
text
Default: Groups silent hain

1. Group me bhejein:
   gon
   → Command auto-delete ho jayegi
   → Ab us group me AI replies dega

2. Band karne ke liye:
   goff
⚠️ Command Tips
Space sahi rakhein: /rate wifi 4500 (theek hai) vs /ratewifi4500 (galat)

Pipe | zaroori hai /addproduct me: /addproduct key | Name | Price

Numbers normalize ho jaate hain: 0300... ya +92300... sab 92300... me convert

Cancel ke 3 alfaaz: cancel, reset, delete — koi bhi chalega

Self chat me test karein pehle, phir customer pe use karein
